  # Correlation Between Keystroke Dynamics On Mobile Devices With Time                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ## Summary
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  MSc thesis at Dublin City University (Supervisor: Prof Alan Smeaton) investigating whether keystroke dynamics on mobile devices vary with time of day. An Android keyboard application (Language Logger) collected 
  344,995 keystrokes from 15 users over 80 days, recording dwell time (key press duration) and flight time (key-to-key transition).
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  Findings: typing dynamics show large variance across users; some users are clearly affected by time of day, others are not. Other latent factors (mood, energy, alertness) likely explain residual variance and are
   flagged as future work.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  Stack: Python, Jupyter, pandas, behavioural-biometrics analysis on a custom-collected dataset.
